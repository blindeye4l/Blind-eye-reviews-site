Community Resources for the Visually Impaired

This is a curated hub of essential tools, technology guides, and advocacy organizations. We've structured these resources to ensure clear, equitable access for everyone.

American Foundation for the Blind (AFB)

Removes barriers, creates solutions, and expands possibilities for people with vision loss.

Visit AFB

Hadley

Personalized learning opportunities/classes on daily living, technology, and braille.

View Classes

APH Connect Center

Offers free advice and resources to assist children, parents, adults, and job seekers who are blind and visually impaired.

Explore Resources

Lighthouse Guild

Dedicated to addressing and preventing vision loss through coordinated care for eye health, vision rehabilitation, and behavioral health.

Get Care

Great Lakes ADA Center

Provides information, materials, technical assistance, and training on the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Visit ADA Center

Be My Eyes

Free app that connects people who are blind or have low vision to sighted volunteers and companies through AI and video.

Learn More

Accessible Pharmacy Services

Comprehensive, home delivery pharmacy service and accessible medication labeling.

Visit Pharmacy

Penny Forward

Financial education and coaching for blind/visually impaired individuals to build financial independence and literacy.

Visit Penny Forward

Caring.com

A leading senior care resource for family caregivers seeking information and support; provides resources for visual impairments.

Visit Caring.com

Bureau of Blind Services (IDHS/DRS)

Offers specialized vocational rehabilitation services and independent living services.

Phone: 217-785-3887

Access Living

Offers Personal Assistants, Housing Assistance, Legal Services, and community support in Chicago.

Visit Access Living

Link Up Illinois (Food Access)

Makes healthy, local, farm-fresh foods more affordable and accessible by doubling the value of Link purchases.

Visit Link Up Illinois

Pace Paratransit (Transportation)

Demand-response, pre-arranged service for eligible riders.

Visit Pace Paratransit

Taxi Assistance Program (TAP)

Provides a subsidy for cab rides within the service area.

Learn about TAP

Rideshare Access Program (RAP)

Provides a subsidy for Uber or UZURV trips within the service area.

Learn about RAP

Opportunities for Ohioans with Disabilities (BSVI)

Assists eligible people with low vision and blindness in finding and retaining meaningful work and personal independence.

Visit OOD

Clovernook Center for the Blind

Promotes independence and high quality of life through employment and comprehensive rehabilitation services.

Visit Clovernook Center

ACB-Ohio Matching Grants Program

Supports blind/visually impaired residents of Ohio to purchase adaptive equipment.

View Grants

Assistive Technology (AT) Ohio

Helps Ohioans of all ages and disabilities learn about or acquire assistive technology.

Visit AT Ohio
